PROJECT OVERVIEW
Design a full marketing website for Social ScaleX, a social media growth agency run by two founders, Nikhil Bisht and Abhishek Anand. The agency manages and grows real Instagram and YouTube accounts for clients across travel, lifestyle, and e-commerce niches. The entire site must use every real number and real client name listed below — no placeholder or invented stats. Visual language is Apple's "Liquid Glass" (visionOS/iOS design system): layered translucent glass surfaces, a bright specular highlight along the top edge of each glass panel, a soft ambient ground shadow implying real thickness, and a subtle inner shadow at the base — never a flat frosted blur. Reference dribbble.com/qclay for overall polish and editorial confidence.
DESIGN SYSTEM
Color palette, psychology-driven:

Violet #7C3AED, primary brand color — creativity and trust
Violet light #A78BFA, secondary tint
Emerald #34D399, growth accent
Emerald dark #059669, for contrast text on light backgrounds
Amber #F59E0B, energy and value accent for CTAs
Paper white #FAF9F6, light section background
Void black #0B0A10, dark section background
Ink #15131C, primary text on light backgrounds
White at varying opacity (90%, 55%, 35%) for text hierarchy on dark backgrounds

Section rhythm alternates light, dark, light, dark, light, dark, light in this order: Hero (light), Marquee (light), Stats band (light), Work/Portfolio (dark), Services (dark), Platforms (dark), Process (dark), Results (light), Why Us (light), Contact/CTA (dark), Footer (light).
Typography: Space Grotesk for all display headlines, bold weight, tight letter-spacing, geometric character. Inter for all body copy, paragraph text, labels, and UI elements, regular to semibold weights.
Glass material construction, apply consistently to every card: base layer is a translucent gradient fill (not a single flat color), roughly 160-degree angle, brighter near the top-left and slightly less opaque toward the bottom; a 1px bright highlight line sits along the very top edge of the card, fading at both corners, to simulate light catching the glass edge; a soft colored ambient shadow sits beneath the card (violet-tinted on light backgrounds, pure black on dark backgrounds); an inner shadow hugs the bottom interior of the card for a sense of depth and thickness.
Interaction pattern to depict in hover/interactive states: cards tilt in 3D perspective toward wherever the cursor is positioned, rotating up to about 7 degrees on each axis, with a soft white radial glare following the cursor position across the card surface, and inner content appearing to float slightly above the glass plane.
Border radius: large, soft, 24px on standard cards, 28px on featured/large cards, fully rounded pills for buttons and tags.

RESPONSIVE REQUIREMENTS
Design three distinct breakpoint layouts, not scaled-down versions of each other:
iPhone (375 to 428px): single column throughout, floating hero stat cards hidden or stacked inline, services list and visual panel stacked vertically, portfolio and results grids single column, full-screen mobile nav drawer.
iPad (768 to 1024px, both orientations): two-column grids for portfolio, platforms, and results, services layout can remain side-by-side if width allows, condensed spacing.
MacBook and desktop (1280px and up): full multi-column bento layouts, floating hero cards visible, sticky "Why Us" left column, maximum visual richness.
MOTION AND PERFORMANCE INTENT
All motion should be achievable with CSS transforms and opacity only — no continuously animated canvas or WebGL backgrounds, no large numbers of independently animated elements competing at once, and all effects must respect reduced-motion preferences. Card hover states use 3D perspective tilt tracking cursor position plus a soft glare, but kept lightweight: a single transform-based interaction per card, not multiple overlapping effects. The goal is a design that looks rich but converts cleanly into smooth, lag-free frontend code with no scroll jank on mid-range devices.
